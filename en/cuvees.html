One Same Standard

Three wines, one same light.

MAISON AURA does not produce three different ranges — it produces a single idea of wine, expressed at three intensities. The Plaisir Cuvée opens without ceremony, the Gastronomie Cuvée is designed for the table, the Grand Exception Cuvée has to be earned. The terroir remains the same from the first bottle to the last; only the patience granted it changes.

Serving

How to enjoy our wines.

A well-served wine speaks differently. A few notes, without dogma, to enjoy it fully.

01

Temperature

Too cold, a wine closes up. Take the bottle out of the fridge fifteen minutes before serving the Plaisir rosés and whites; the Gastronomie and Exception cuvées benefit from being opened a little earlier, at cellar temperature.

02

The Glass

A tulip glass, not too wide, is enough to reveal the essentials. For the Gastronomie and Exception reds, a Bordeaux glass gives the wine room to open.

03

Decanting

Unnecessary for Brise Marine. For Terroir, thirty minutes in a decanter softens young reds. For AURA TRÈS, aeration happens in the glass, slowly — it's part of the pleasure.
